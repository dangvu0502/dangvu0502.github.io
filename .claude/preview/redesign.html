I started in 2022 as a developer trainee at FPT Software. That autumn I joined Bravebits to work on PageFly, a Shopify page builder that 200,000+ stores use to build their storefronts.

Three years there. The work I am proudest of is the performance track: merchants' pages were slow, Shopify's Built for Shopify program set hard limits, and I owned getting under them. Initial load came down 20% and LCP held under 2.5s across merchant storefronts. Later I built Image-to-PageFly, which turns a screenshot of any design into an editable page in under a minute, and Figma2PageFly, a one-click import from Figma.

Since November 2025 I have been at sending.ac, a smaller team where I own whole features end to end: Stripe billing with subscriptions, coupons and invoicing, Logto single sign-on across the product, and a sequencer integration that runs 200+ automated workflows a day.

On the side I contribute upstream to tensorzero and nextest, and I rebuilt this site as a Next.js static export, 165 KB, Lighthouse 100. The previous version was Rust compiled to WASM.
